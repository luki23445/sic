Antifoaming agents

Antifoaming agents, commonly called "defoamers", are used to eliminate foam and prevent its formation. Our range of antifoaming agents is the result of an ambitious development program undertaken to develop highly efficient antifoaming agents for various industries including:

  • potato industry (starch, fries, chips production),
  • sugar industry,
  • fruit and vegetable processing industry,
  • wood industry,
  • paints and varnishes industry,
  • coke plants,
  • chemical plants,
  • wastewater treatment plants (municipal and industrial).

These agents have been developed based on the latest knowledge and global development trends in this field. The high effectiveness of the agents has been confirmed in many factories and industrial plants throughout the country. The agents have been successfully tested in various technological systems, and their effectiveness and efficiency is in some cases several times higher compared to other similar agents available on the European market. High efficiency is manifested by achieving an immediate defoaming effect with the use of a relatively small unit dose.
